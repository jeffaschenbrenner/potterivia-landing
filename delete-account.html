Delete Your Account

Last updated: July 7, 2026

You can permanently delete your Potterivia account and all associated data at any time, either from within the app or by contacting us directly.

Option 1: Delete In-App

  1. Open Potterivia and sign in
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Scroll down and select Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Your account is deleted immediately and permanently — this cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have the app installed, or can't sign in, email us at hello@potterivia.com from the email address associated with your account and request deletion. We'll delete your account and confirm once it's done.

What Gets Deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account credentials (email, login access)
  • Your profile — username, display name, chosen house, profile icon
  • Your gameplay history — scores, streaks, answered questions, badges and titles earned
  • Your entries on the leaderboard and House Cup standings

This data is removed right away and is not recoverable after deletion.

Questions

If you have any questions about account deletion or what data we hold, see our Privacy Policy or contact us at hello@potterivia.com.
