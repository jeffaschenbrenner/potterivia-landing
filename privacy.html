Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026

Potterivia ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — when you create an account, we collect your email address and a username you choose.
  • Gameplay data — your trivia answers, scores, house assignment, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Profile data — your chosen Hogwarts house, titles earned (Prefect, Head Student, etc.), and game history.
  • Waitlist email — if you sign up for launch notifications on our website, we store your email address.

We do not collect payment information, precise location data, contacts, or any sensitive personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To operate the game, including tracking scores and house points for the House Cup leaderboard
  • To send you launch and update notifications if you opted in via the waitlist form
  • To improve the app and fix issues

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising or marketing beyond communications you explicitly opted into.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Potterivia:

  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider. Your account data and game data are stored on Supabase infrastructure. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic — used to generate trivia questions. No personal user data is sent to Anthropic. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree — used to process waitlist email signups on our website. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is deleted within 30 days. Waitlist emails are deleted once the app has launched and notifications have been sent.

5. Children's Privacy

Potterivia is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for waitlist emails at any time by contacting us

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@potterivia.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at hello@potterivia.com.
