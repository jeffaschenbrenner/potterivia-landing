How It Works

Play. Progress. Earn your place in wizarding history.

🤖

AI-Crafted Questions

Questions are generated live by Claude, Anthropic's AI — so no two games are ever the same. Across 10 categories, from Spells to Horcruxes.

⏱

Speed Matters

Answer fast, earn more. Each correct answer gives base points × difficulty multiplier plus up to 50 speed bonus points.

🏆

The House Cup

Every month a House Cup season runs. Houses are ranked by points-per-active-wizard — so a small, dedicated house can beat a larger one.

🎓

Seven Years at Hogwarts

Compete in each monthly House Cup to advance from Year 1 through Year 7 — just like a real Hogwarts student. You can only progress one year per season.

⭐

Earn Your Status

The top performers each season are awarded Prefect or Perfect status badges. Reach Year 7 and top the global standings to become Head Student.

📜

Shape the Vault

Earn enough prestige and you unlock the ability to submit your own questions to the trivia vault — directly shaping the game for the whole community.

🎯

Choose Your Difficulty

Easy, Medium, Hard, or let the Sorting Hat decide. Harder questions earn more points, but the clock is always ticking.

📖

Learn As You Play

Every answer comes with an AI-written explanation, so even a wrong answer teaches you something new about the wizarding world.

🔥

Streak Tracking

String together perfect games to build your streak. Your best streak is recorded forever on your wizarding profile.
