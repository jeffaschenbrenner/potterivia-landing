How It Works

Every question is a fresh challenge

🤖

AI-Crafted Questions

Questions are generated live by Claude, Anthropic's AI — so no two games are ever the same. Across 10 categories, from Spells to Horcruxes.

⏱

Speed Matters

Answer fast, earn more. Each correct answer gives base points × difficulty multiplier plus up to 50 speed bonus points.

🏆

The House Cup

Your points roll up to your house's running total. Compete globally — as an individual and as part of your house — on live leaderboards.

🎯

Choose Your Difficulty

Easy, Medium, Hard, or let the Sorting Hat decide. Harder questions earn more points, but the clock is always ticking.

📖

Learn As You Play

Every answer comes with an AI-written explanation, so even a wrong answer teaches you something new about the wizarding world.

🔥

Streak Tracking

String together perfect games to build your streak. Your best streak is recorded forever on your wizarding profile.
